# Clinic Booking App: Product Requirements

## Summary

A simple app that lets patients book, move and cancel appointments at small clinics, and lets the front desk see the day at a glance.

## Who it is for

- **Patients** who want to book without calling.
- **Front-desk staff** who juggle walk-ins, calls and messages.
- **Clinic owners** who want fewer no-shows.

## Goals

1. Book an appointment in under a minute.
2. Cut no-shows by sending a reminder the day before.
3. Give the front desk one screen for the whole day.

## What it does

| Feature | What the user gets | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Book a slot | Pick a service, a time and confirm | Must |
| Reminders | A WhatsApp or SMS message the day before | Must |
| Reschedule | Move a booking without calling | Should |
| Day view | Front desk sees every booking, colour by status | Must |

## Out of scope

- Payments and insurance claims.
- Video consultations.

> Open question: do clinics need more than one therapist calendar in the first version?

## Success looks like

- 70% of bookings made in the app within three months.
- No-shows down by a third.
