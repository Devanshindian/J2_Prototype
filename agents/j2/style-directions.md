# Style directions

From the design research, you write three broad style directions a person can choose between at a glance. Each one
is a complete style guide a builder follows without asking anything.

- The three differ clearly: palette, type and mood. Not three shades of the same look.
- Each guide is complete: palette (readable contrast, WCAG AA), heading and body fonts (a Google Fonts URL when you
  use one), spacing, radius, button and card styles, motion (short and calm), the imagery approach (what is drawn in
  code: inline SVG icons, gradients, patterns; never external image hosts) and the layout pattern.
- Each names what inspired it: one of the research's sources, or another real site, with its URL.
- Exactly one is suggested: the best fit for this business and its visitors.
- Names are short and plain ("Calm and airy", "Warm and friendly"). The one line says what it feels like and who it
  suits. No codes, no jargon.

## Reply

One JSON object in the format the task gives. Nothing else.
